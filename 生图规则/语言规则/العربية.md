# العربية

规则版本：v1

## Language Scope

This rule reuses the English storyboard, evidence, and layout baseline. It controls only newly added visible marketing copy and its reading direction.

## Required

- All newly added display names, headlines, subheads, feature labels, and callouts must use natural Arabic only.
- Localize the English copy intent for Arabic-speaking ecommerce readers; never render the English source text.
- Use right-to-left reading order, right-aligned hierarchy, correct joined Arabic letter shaping, and native punctuation.
- Mirror directional copy layouts and callout reading flow when needed, but do not mirror the product, logo, or packaging itself.
- Preserve original text already printed on the uploaded product or packaging; it is exempt from the language rule.
- Keep product facts, claims, scene diversity, and visual evidence identical to the English baseline.

## Forbidden

- Do not mix newly added English, Chinese, disconnected Arabic glyphs, random text, or internal instructions into the marketing copy.
- Do not invent specifications, certifications, prices, ratings, or claims during localization.

## Review

- If newly added visible copy is not uniformly natural, correctly shaped, right-to-left Arabic, mark the result as low quality or failed.
