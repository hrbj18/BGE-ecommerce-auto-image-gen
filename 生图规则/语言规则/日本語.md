# 日本語

规则版本：v1

## Language Scope

This rule reuses the English storyboard, evidence, and layout baseline. It controls only newly added visible marketing copy.

## Required

- All newly added display names, headlines, subheads, feature labels, and callouts must use natural Japanese only.
- Translate the English copy intent for Japanese ecommerce readers; never render the English source text.
- Use idiomatic Japanese wording, punctuation, and line breaks rather than literal machine-translation phrasing.
- Preserve original text already printed on the uploaded product or packaging; it is exempt from the language rule.
- Keep product facts, claims, scene diversity, and visual evidence identical to the English baseline.

## Forbidden

- Do not mix newly added English, Chinese, Korean, romaji, random characters, or internal instructions into the marketing copy.
- Do not invent specifications, certifications, prices, ratings, or claims during localization.

## Review

- If newly added visible copy is not uniformly natural Japanese, mark the result as low quality or failed.
